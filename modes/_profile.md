# User Profile Context — Seattle data roles

## Target roles
Senior Data Analyst, Analytics Engineer, BI Analyst and related individual-contributor roles.
Use these archetypes instead of the upstream AI/LLMOps/FDE archetypes.
Prioritize data quality, root-cause analysis, reliable reporting, internal operations analytics,
measurement, validation and stakeholder collaboration. Pure dashboard production is a lower preference.
Consider institutional research and program evaluation where analytical responsibilities fit.

## Location policy
Home base: Kirkland, Washington. No relocation.
Prioritize Seattle, Bellevue, Kirkland and Redmond; Bothell and Issaquah are secondary nearby options.
US remote roles must permit a Washington-based employee. "Remote" alone is not confirmation.
Review office frequency and commute separately for all hybrid roles.
Olympia, Tumwater, Mountlake Terrace, SeaTac and other places outside the core cities are
commute-review items, not automatically approved local matches.

## Seniority and employer preferences
Prefer IC roles and mature teams. Avoid required people management.
Do not infer management solely from Manager or Lead in a title; read the duties.
Prioritize government, education, healthcare, insurance, utilities and established enterprises.
Keep local technology employers as a second priority and national remote employers as a separate pool.
Sector or employer size is not evidence of work-life balance; assess team-level evidence.

## Compensation and authorization
No sponsorship needed. Do not exclude postings that do not sponsor.
No confirmed salary floor is configured; do not inherit the example profile's $120K minimum.
Flag contract duration, part-time hours, weekend shifts and travel for review rather than silently rejecting them.

## Evidence boundary
These files configure targeting, not a complete CV.
Do not invent work history, credentials, contact details, proof points or salary expectations.
Read the user's cv.md before generating application materials.
